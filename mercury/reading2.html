Dear Huda,

I hope this email finds you well. You asked me to tell you about the summer course I took in Global Leadership in São Paulo, Brazil last month. It is like the programme your sister took in Rio de Janeiro last year. I took the course in order to equip myself with the knowledge and skills I need to succeed in culturally diverse workplaces. After completing the course, I got a job at Buenos Aires College in Argentina.

The course was for seven days. It started on Sunday and finished on Saturday. Though I was excited to join, I started the course one day late on Monday due to bad weather. It was a blended learning course, with online and in-person components. The online elements of the course were taught using the Coursera Learning System (CLS). These included four online discussion sessions, individual assignments and five face-to-face sessions which students had to attend to be awarded their final certificate.

My class was a mixture of different professions and nationalities. We had academic researchers, lab technicians, hotel managers and college tutors like myself. The participants’ various backgrounds and experiences added to the flavour of our class. The majority were Brazilian but we also had some participants from Mexico, Spain, and the UK.

The programme organisers aimed to host all the international participants with local families. Unfortunately, the Spanish students and I had to live in hostels due to our late arrival. Imagine some of the students had to be accommodated in hotels because they refused to stay with local families! I would have loved to have stayed with a Brazilian family.

Throughout the course, we were trained to ask critical questions, respond thoughtfully to the issues we both observed and encountered when dealing with people. One big problem I had was how to deal with unexpected situations. We also had the opportunity to improve our skills in essential areas such as project management, teamwork and intercultural communication. What was good about the course was that sufficient time was given to students to focus on their assigned tasks and projects. Unfortunately, some participants failed because they did not actively contribute to the online discussions which of course were all in English.

Thank you for taking the time to read about the course. I look forward to sharing more stories and experiences with you in the near future.

Warm regards,
Susanna
