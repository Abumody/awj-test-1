Jabal Ahad (Mount Uhud)

Jabal Ahad, also known as Mount Uhud, is one of the most famous mountains in Saudi Arabia. It is located about 5 kilometers north of the Prophet's Mosque in Madinah. The mountain stands at a height of approximately 1,077 meters above sea level. It is not the tallest mountain in the region, but its historical and religious importance makes it a popular destination for visitors.

The mountain is best known for the Battle of Uhud, which took place in 625 CE (3 AH) between the early Muslims and the army of Mecca. Many important events happened near this mountain, and it is mentioned in many historical texts. Today, visitors come to Jabal Ahad to see the graves of the martyrs who died in the battle. The mountain itself is made of dark red rock and has a distinct shape that can be seen from far away.

For those who want to climb the mountain, there are several paths to the top. The climb is not very difficult, but visitors should be careful because the rocks can be slippery. Many people walk up the mountain to enjoy the view of Madinah from above. Others prefer to use vehicles on the wider paths. The best time to visit is during early morning or late afternoon when the weather is cooler. In summer, temperatures can be very high, so visitors should bring water and wear sun protection.

Visitors should also be aware that there are no restaurants or shops on the mountain itself. It is a good idea to bring your own food and drinks. Comfortable walking shoes are strongly recommended because the ground is uneven. Also, visitors should respect the religious importance of the area and avoid loud behavior. For those who cannot climb, there are flat areas near the base where you can sit and learn about the history of the battle from local guides.
