Medical Tourism

Medical tourism has become very popular nowadays. A recent report by ‘Patients Beyond Borders’, an international health travel magazine, shows that nearly eight million patients travelled abroad for medical care in 2019. Medical tourism is big business, and the value of this industry is between twenty and forty billion US dollars. In addition, a report by the ‘World Medical Tourism Market’ forecast that the medical tourism industry will grow by 25% by 2026.

According to the ‘Global Health Travel’ magazine report in 2020, the rapid growth in medical tourism is due to several reasons. The low cost in countries such as India, Thailand and Turkey is one of the reasons. For example, the surgery prices are 30% to 70% lower in these countries compared to others. The second reason is the easy access to online consumer information about medical treatments around the world. This helps people make decisions about the most suitable hospitals and they can book appointments without getting delayed or being placed on a waiting list. Many websites are marketing best medical centres and are available to serve people who are looking for medical services in different countries. For example, one of the famous free sites is Medigo, which was launched in 2014, in Germany. This site lists over two hundred hospitals in twelve different countries such as the UK, USA and China, and is available in four languages. However, according to the ‘Global Health Travel’ statistics, the main reason that attracts medical tourists is the quality of the medical services provided by well-trained doctors in technologically advanced hospitals.

According to the list of the most popular medical destinations published by ‘Patients Beyond Borders’ in 2019, Malaysia comes at the top of the list. It is famous for luxurious hospitals with five-star rooms and it offers medical treatments for people who struggle to have children. Brazil is second on the list with approximately three hundred thousand medical travellers. It has the highest number of cosmetic surgeons in the world. Even film and TV stars have operations in this country. India is the third most popular destination, and is home for highly trained heart doctors at a low cost. Although Singapore is very expensive, it comes fourth on the list because it has one of the most advanced healthcare systems in the world with the highest technology especially for cancer treatments. Turkey has a growing medical tourism sector, and it is fifth on the list. It is known for its eye treatments, especially laser surgery. Turkey also offers discounted flights through its airlines to attract more medical tourists.

With the rise of medical tourism, destinations that offer quality and affordable healthcare, competition between countries has increased to provide top quality medical services with attractive offers.
