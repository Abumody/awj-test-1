THE PALACE ON WHEELS

The Palace on Wheels is one of the top ten luxury train journeys in the world. It started operating in India on the 26th of January 1982 and is managed by India’s National Railways. It runs once a week from September to April each year for eight days and seven nights. To avoid the monsoon season and the summer heat, it is recommended to go on this trip between the months of October and March.

The Palace on Wheels’ goal is to provide 24-hour service to make every passenger feel as if they are kings and queens. This train has many modern facilities. There are 23 coaches on the train, and it can host up to a maximum of 104 passengers. Each coach has four cabins with luxury amenities such as individually designed single and double cabins, ensuite bathroom, wall to wall carpeting, entertainment system, Wi-Fi and personal assistants who do everything for their passengers, like making the bed, doing the laundry, etc. The train has two restaurants, The Maharaja, and The Maharani, that serve Indian, International, and Chinese cuisine. It has a magnificent lounge, library, and spa.

The train’s journey is a seven-night, eight-day round trip that starts and finishes in Delhi, the capital of India. Passengers boarding the Palace on Wheels generally arrive in the afternoon at Safdarjung Railway Station where they are welcomed onto the train with flowers. The train departs at 18.30. While passengers are sleeping in their luxury cabins, the train arrives in Jaipur, the capital of Rajasthan. It is also known as the 'Pink City'. After breakfast on day two, passengers have the entire day to explore the palace as well as the fort. On day three, passengers will find a very different type of adventure awaiting them. Stepping off the train at Sawai Madhopur, they are driven to the hills and forests of Ranthambore National Park for a safari in tiger country where these majestic animals can even be seen during the day.

The following morning, the train arrives in Udaipur, often referred to as the ‘City of Lakes’. That night, passengers can experience a thrilling firework display from a traditional boat on one of Udaipur’s beautiful lakes. The next stop, on day five, is the historic fort town of Jaisalmer. After lunch in a 7-star hotel, passengers are taken on a camel ride into the surrounding dunes where they enjoy displays of local music and dance as the sun sets.

The following day, the train arrives at Jodhpur. This is a fabulous city full of palaces, forts, and blue-painted houses that passengers can view seated on top of majestic elephants. The next day is spent in Agra, home to the fabulous Taj Mahal, one of the new seven wonders of the world. That evening, passengers are treated to a spectacular sound and light show followed by a feast on board the train. On the final day, day 8, passengers say farewell to the staff who have done everything to make their time on board, the Palace on Wheels, their most memorable ever.
