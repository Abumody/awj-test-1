Essay Question

A survey of students was conducted to find out which game they like. The pie chart below shows the results of their preferences. Summarize the information in the pie chart by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant. Your writing should be clear and well organized.
Conditions: -
Minimum words: 100
Model Answer (shown after submission):
The survey results show that football is the most popular game among students, with 600 people choosing it. Hockey comes next with 500 students, followed by golf with 400. Cricket and baseball are equally liked, each attracting 300 students. Rugby is less popular, with 200 students preferring it, while basketball is the least favored, chosen by only 100 students. From these figures, it is clear that team sports such as football and hockey dominate students’ interests, while individual or less common games like rugby and basketball attract fewer fans. The difference between football and basketball is especially striking, as football is six times more popular. Overall, the survey highlights that football is the leading choice, while basketball remains the least preferred among the group.
