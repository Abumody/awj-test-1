Essay Question

A survey was conducted to find out which countries consumed the most rice in 2011. The chart below shows the results of this survey. Summarize the information in the chart by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant. Your writing should be clear and well organized.
Conditions: -
Minimum words: 100
Model Answer (shown after submission):
The chart illustrates rice consumption in the top ten countries in 2011. China recorded the highest consumption, reaching nearly 120 million metric tonnes. India followed closely with just under 100 million tonnes. Indonesia and Bangladesh were also significant consumers, with figures around 40–50 million tonnes. The remaining countries—Vietnam, the Philippines, Myanmar, Japan, Thailand, and Brazil—consumed much smaller amounts, ranging between 10 and 30 million tonnes each. Among these, Vietnam had the highest, while Brazil had the lowest. Overall, the data shows that Asian countries dominate global rice consumption, with China and India far ahead of all others. In contrast, non-Asian countries like Brazil consume comparatively little, highlighting the importance of rice as a staple food in Asia.
