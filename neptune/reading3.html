Trusted Media Brands

Trusted Media Brands, previously known as the ‘Reader's Digest Association’, is an American multi-digital platform media and publishing company. The Reader's Digest Association company was founded by DeWitt Wallace, and his wife Lila Bell, in New York City in 1922. Later, the name of the company was changed to Trusted Media Brands in September, 2015 by Bonnie Kintzer, the Chief Content Officer (CCO). The new name was chosen to include the various brands of the company under one umbrella rather than just 'Reader's Digest'. The company now contains ten well-known brands which are ‘Taste of Home’, ‘Reader’s Digest’, ‘The Healthy’, ‘Family Handyman’, ‘Birds & Blooms’, ‘Country’, ‘Country Woman’, ‘Farm & Ranch Living’, ‘Reminisce’ and ‘Haven Home Media’, which is the latest.

Trusted Media Brands has continued to publish ‘Reader’s Digest’ magazine with its inspiring true stories and up-to-date information. It reaches an average audience of nineteen million. It contains fifty editions translated into twenty different languages. These editions include Indian, Australian, Canadian, Portuguese, Swedish and many more. At one time, there was an Arabic edition, titled ‘Al Mukhtar’, which was published in Egypt. However, it was stopped in 1993 by the ‘Reader’s Digest Association’.

According to Trusted Media Brands CCO, during the COVID 19 pandemic, the most visited and electronically read magazine is the ‘Taste of Home’ where the number of visitors reached more than 32.3 million. The ‘Family Handyman’ is the second most widely read magazine with 9.6 million readers. Bonnie Kintzer stated that the reason for this increase was that the company works hard to create engaging content to support the growing numbers of readers of all ages who want to break the boredom of having to stay at home during COVID 19. For example, people appreciate the ‘Taste of Home' as it provides them with new home recipes. It also provides them with information to help them store their food supplies smartly. The ‘Family Handyman’ supports families with online courses on how to repair their homes and these are suitable for all ages.

All in all, the Trusted Media Brands' administration board is very efficient and powerful. The company operates about 60 branded websites. It uses its database of more than 140 million consumers to market books, special-interest magazines, music, videos, financial and health products in 76 countries. According to recent statistics, 80 million visitors a month log into the Trusted Media Brands websites in addition to the 16 million people who read the paper magazine.
