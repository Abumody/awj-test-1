Dear Majid,

I hope you’re doing well. How is it at Dhofar University? You haven’t written to me for a while. I expect you must be busy with your studies. Anyway, I wanted to tell you about the summer course I took at Sultan Qaboos University (SQU). It was similar to the programme your brother, Sami, took at Nizwa University last year. It was a fantastic experience, and I know you'll be interested to hear about it.

The course was for a total of eight weeks, four of which were spent in face-to-face and online sessions at SQU. It started the last week of June and finished the third week of August. Unfortunately, I joined the course one week later than scheduled in July because I wasn’t well. There were three courses: Accounting, Marketing and Management and we had to select one according to our specialisation and interest. I chose the Marketing Course as I felt it would help me get a good job after graduation.

An important part of the course involved being placed in a business environment for three weeks. The reason SQU did this was to equip students with the skills they needed to work with business organisations and experience first-hand real world problems. I was selected to work with a company called Falcon while some of my classmates went to Shell and others to Majan. As a result, we acquired essential skills in key areas such as customer service, cooperation, discipline and report writing. However, the most important thing I learned was how to manage my time more efficiently. As you know I find it difficult to get things done on time!

Initially, the most challenging problem we all had was adapting to business working hours especially those of us who lived outside Muscat. We had to work two shifts from 9 am to 1 pm and then work from 4 pm to 7 pm. It was so different to studying at SQU where we finished at 2 pm every day. The last week of the course was very busy as we only had five days to write, edit and submit our projects. I was really happy when I got an ‘A’ in my final report. However, I felt very sorry for those who failed as they won’t be allowed to graduate. I thought it was because they had missed some classes or had not attended their online sessions but it was because they had submitted their work late after the deadline had passed.

Please, write soon and tell me all your news.

Best regards,
Ahmed
